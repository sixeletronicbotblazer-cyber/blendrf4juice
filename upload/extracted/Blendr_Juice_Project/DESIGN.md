# Blendr — visual direction

## Brand idea

A compact bottle blender in a daily wellness routine. The supplied real photographs carry the product: a bottle in a person's hand, alongside fruit and smoothie scenes. The look is warm, fresh and polished without a clinical or cartoon finish.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#18252D` | Headlines, body copy, button shadow |
| Cream | `#F9F5EE` | Main warm background |
| Soft cream | `#FFFAF4` | Light sections and cards |
| Mint | `#D8F4ED` | How-it-works section |
| Blue mist | `#95B8BD` | Moving drinks ticker |
| Violet | `#7950C5` | Primary CTA, announcement, accent copy |
| Deep violet | `#6440AD` | CTA hover |
| Blush | `#F7DCE1` | Pink product accent and gift atmosphere |
| Pale green | `#E9F4ED` | Lifestyle routine section |

Color balance: cream and photography dominate, violet is reserved for action and emphasis, mint and blush distinguish sections. The blue verification badge comes from the supplied Blendr mark, not a new accent applied throughout the page.

## Typography and components

- Headlines and body: DM Sans, bold headlines with tight spacing. CTA labels: Outfit. System sans fallback is included.
- Logo: supplied Blendr wordmark, kept as an image. The small blue verification badge remains part of the logo.
- Pills: white or pale surfaces, fully rounded; badges are short and readable.
- CTA: violet gradient capsule, white text and a round arrow medallion; lifts slightly on hover. The final next-page CTAs have a subtle pulse.
- Benefits: soft rectangular cards with violet icons and a gentle lift on hover.
- Sections: generous spacing on desktop and stacked, readable flow on mobile.

## Image direction

- The movement and routine panels use supplied real product photographs with the original pixels preserved. The layout crop removes the promotional captions at the bottom.
- The pink and ivory product photos supplied by the brand remain in the color selection section.
- The person holds the bottle body. Model 214 is 82 × 82 × 218 mm and 490 g; those dimensions guide any future photos with people. Keep the cap and loop visible.
- The three supplied demonstrations retain their 550:691 source ratio. On mobile each GIF gets its full frame in a vertical card; desktop uses three columns.
- Two new responsive hero scenes show the pink bottle with strawberries and the ivory bottle with kiwi. Their FRESH JUICE labels mirror the physical product reference.
- The details section uses a transparent PNG of the pink bottle against a blush glow. The offer cards frame the complete supplied pink and ivory product photographs in square panels. The eBook gift has a dedicated cover.

## Motion

- The JUICES / SMOOTHIES / SHAKES strip loops continuously, pauses on hover, and stays static for reduced-motion settings.
- The hero slides laterally between two scenes every 3.3 seconds, with manual dots and a pause on hover/focus. The 20k+ and 5k+ figures count up once on entering view.
- Sections fade in as they enter view. Buttons and benefit cards lift on hover.
- Motion is supplementary: content and calls to action remain usable without it.

## Content flow

Hero → moving drinks strip → energy and lifestyle → three product steps → design → nine benefits → daily routine → product details → two colors and eBook gift → community proof → final CTA.
